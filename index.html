OVERVIEW

Overview of FOMI. The force-feedback manipulation interface incorporates a scissor-shaped mechanism to provide human operators with intuitive force feedback in robot-free collection of contact-rich demonstrations, while also supporting teleoperation for policy learning.

Mechanical Design For Intuitive Force Feedback

Actuation links in FOMI’s scissor-shaped mechanism transmit object interaction forces directly to the handles without force sensors, providing intuitive force feedback, which supports real-time adjustment in contact-rich tasks. Meshed sector gears synchronize gripper opening and closing, while the handles support the interface during manipulation.

Demonstration Collection

FOMI supports robot-free collection through sensor calibration, data acquisition, and synchronization, and enables teleoperation by mapping pose and gripper measurements to robot commands. Teleoperation relies on visual feedback; mechanical stops indicate gripper width command limits without conveying object interaction forces.

Results

Demonstration Collection Performance

In an exploratory study, three operators with no prior robot operation experience used each interface on all four tasks. FOMI achieved the highest mean collection efficiency on each task and the highest overall completion rate in both robot-free collection and teleoperation.

Policy Performance

We fine-tune the π0.5 vision-language-action (VLA) model separately on robot-free and teleoperated demonstrations. The policies achieve average success rates of 78.4% with 100 robot-free demonstrations per task and 70.5% with 25 teleoperated demonstrations per task.
